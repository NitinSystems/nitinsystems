### Section 6 — How We Approach Systems (Methodology)
1. **01 — Understand:** Clarify the business process, bottlenecks, and requirements.
2. **02 — Map:** Define data structures, triggers, conditions, handoffs, and dependencies.
3. **03 — Design:** Map the workflow architecture before building.
4. **04 — Build:** Implement the required automation scenarios and integrations.
5. **05 — Test:** Validate the system against real scenarios and edge cases.
6. **06 — Handoff:** Document the system and make the workflow understandable to the people operating it.

- **Documentation:** A comprehensive walkthrough of what was built and how it operates.
