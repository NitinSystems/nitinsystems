SYSTEMS-FIRST METHODOLOGY

How I Approach Systems

A structured process to ensure every build is reliable, documented, and understandable before it goes live.

STAGE 01

Understand the Business

Before touching any tool, I clarify the actual process. Who triggers the action? Where does data get lost? What does the team do manually every day?

STAGE 02

Map Data & Dependencies

Every field, trigger, and branching condition is mapped visually. I account for missing data, duplicate entries, and failure states early.

STAGE 03

Design the Architecture

The workflow blueprint, data schemas, and CRM structure are designed completely before any scenario is built.

STAGE 04

Build Scenarios & Integrations

I build each automation with custom error handling, automatic retry logic, and instant team alerts so no lead or record is ever lost silently.

STAGE 05

Validate Under Real Conditions

Live payload testing, duplicate submission simulations, and timeout verification to confirm everything holds under real-world conditions.

STAGE 06

Document & Hand Off

Recorded Loom walkthroughs and architecture schematics delivered so your team fully understands how the system runs and scales.
