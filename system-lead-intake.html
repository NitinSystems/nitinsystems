01 THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

How Duplicate Leads Break Sales Pipelines

When growing service businesses run ads, webinars, and website forms simultaneously, prospects frequently submit multiple forms with slight variations (e.g., personal vs. work email, formatted vs. raw phone numbers). Without centralized deduplication, CRMs become bloated with duplicate contacts, causing reps to cross wires and send conflicting follow-ups.

❌ FRACTURED LEAD HISTORIES

Deal notes, consultation notes, and form submissions split across multiple records for the same client.

❌ REVENUE ATTRIBUTION BLIND SPOTS

Marketing cannot accurately track campaign ROI because duplicate records mask original source attribution.

03 DATA FORMATTING ENGINE

Data Formatting & Clean Ingestion Engine

Before any database query occurs, incoming payloads pass through an automated sanitizer that ensures clean data formatting:

01 · EMAIL CANONICALIZATION

Trims leading/trailing whitespace, downcases characters, and strips plus-addressing aliases (e.g. name+test@domain.com).

02 · E.164 PHONE STANDARDIZATION

Parses country code prefixes, strips dashes/parentheses, and formats phone numbers into international E.164 standard.

03 · DOMAIN & COMPANY ENRICHMENT

Extracts corporate root domain names from email addresses to automatically link leads to existing account records.

04 MAKE.COM AUTOMATION MODULES

3-Tier Automation Architecture

The system executes through 3 distinct Make.com modules to eliminate single points of failure:

MODULE 01 · INTAKE & SANITIZE
Webhook Ingestion Router

Receives real-time payloads from web forms, validates required parameters, and logs the raw transaction to the audit ledger.

MODULE 02 · MATCH & TRIAGE
Airtable Relational Indexer

Queries existing records on canonical email, normalized phone, and company domain. Determines whether to create, update, or append history.

MODULE 03 · CRM SYNCHRONIZATION
HubSpot Contact & Deal Router

Pushes verified records into HubSpot CRM, associates incoming submissions with existing deals, and notifies assigned sales reps.

05 DATA GOVERNANCE

3-Table Relational Airtable Schema

Database governance is maintained across 3 structured tables to ensure an immutable record of every lead interaction:

Table 01: Raw Inbound Ledger

Immutable log of all raw webhook payloads, source UTM parameters, and timestamps.

Table 02: Master Verified Contacts

Single source of truth containing sanitized emails, normalized phones, and linked account IDs.

Table 03: Duplicate Audit Log

Historical record tracking matched fields, merge actions, and multi-touch submission counts.
