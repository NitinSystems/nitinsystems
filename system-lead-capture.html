Lead Capture to Booking System

An automated inbound lead qualification and scheduling pipeline engineered for an active service business: replacing manual email coordination with real-time 2-way Google Calendar synchronization, custom intake routing, and automated SMS reminders.

Core CRM & Workflow
GoHighLevel (GHL)
Pipeline triggers & custom tags
Calendar Integration
Google Calendar 2-Way
Live conflict avoidance & buffer logic
Communication Layer
SMS & Email Sequences
Instant confirmation & 24h/1h reminders
Production Delivery: Built and deployed directly into live business operations for a service company managing multi-rep consultation schedules.
01 THE OPERATIONAL PROBLEM

Inbox Friction & Scheduling Latency

Prior to implementation, incoming website inquiries were delivered as static email notifications to a shared inbox. Sales representatives had to manually reply, exchange multiple back-and-forth emails to confirm availability, and manually create calendar events.

❌ 4–12 HOUR RESPONSE DELAYS

Leads submitted interest during off-hours and were contacted hours later, dramatically dropping call show-up rates.

❌ DOUBLE-BOOKING & UNTRACKED LEADS

Reps occasionally scheduled conflicting meetings, while non-responsive leads fell out of the pipeline without follow-up.

05 SYSTEM RELIABILITY

Reliability Safeguards & Edge Cases

To ensure 100% operational uptime and prevent appointment drop-offs, the pipeline was built with specific fallback mechanisms:

Time-Zone Normalization

Automatically detects prospect browser time zone and locks calendar invites in ISO 8601 format.

Cancellation & Reschedule Webhooks

If a prospect reschedules, the pipeline frees up the slot immediately and updates the CRM stage.

Multi-Channel Reminder Cadence

Automated email at 24h and SMS at 1h prior to call, reducing no-show rates.
