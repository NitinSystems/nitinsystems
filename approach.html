SYSTEMS-FIRST METHODOLOGY

How I Approach Systems

Automation isn't just connecting apps; it is designing a repeatable, practical, reliable operating process that you and your team can trust.

STAGE 01

Understand the Business Context

Artifact: Process Discovery Matrix

Before opening Make.com or touching CRM settings, I clarify the commercial process with you directly. Who triggers the action? What information is required? Where are team members spending manual hours coordinating?

Current Bottleneck MapTool Dependency AuditStakeholder Interviews
STAGE 02

Map Data & Dependencies

Artifact: Visual Data Flow Chart

I map every field, trigger, webhook payload, and branching condition visually. I account for missing data fields, duplicate contact entries, and potential error states early.

Trigger & Payload SchemasDeduplication RulesBranching Decision Trees
STAGE 03

Design Workflow Architecture

Artifact: Architecture Blueprint

I establish structured data models in Airtable or HubSpot, define integration dependencies, and construct clear architecture schematics that you and your team can easily review and understand.

Relational Database DesignCRM Property MappingError Handling Directives
STAGE 04

Build Scenarios & Integrations

Artifact: Production Scenario Modules

I engineer the workflows directly in Make.com, GoHighLevel, or Zapier, building custom error-handling paths, automatic retry logic, and real-time team alerts so that no lead or record is ever dropped silently.

Make.com & Zapier ModulesWebhook HandlersInstant Failure Alerts
STAGE 05

Validate Under Real Scenarios

Artifact: Verification & Test Log

I run rigorous live payload testing across every scenario: rapid duplicate submissions, malformed email strings, and edge-case failure simulations to verify fail-safe behavior.

Edge-Case TestingTimeout & Retry VerificationPayload Integrity Checks
STAGE 06

Document & Operational Handoff

Artifact: Loom Guides & Handover Doc

I record and deliver detailed Loom video walkthroughs along with clear architectural schematics so you and your team have clear understanding of how the system operates and can maintain it after handoff.

Recorded Loom WalkthroughsArchitecture SchematicsTeam Ownership Guides
