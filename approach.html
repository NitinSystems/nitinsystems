SYSTEMS-FIRST METHODOLOGY

How We Approach Systems

Automation isn't just connecting apps; it is designing a repeatable, fault-tolerant operating process that your team can trust.

STAGE 01

Understand the Business Context

Artifact: Process Discovery Matrix

Before opening Make.com or touching CRM settings, we clarify the commercial process. Who triggers the action? What information is required? Where are team members spending manual hours coordinating?

Current Bottleneck MapTool Dependency AuditStakeholder Interviews
STAGE 02

Map Data & Dependencies

Artifact: Visual Data Flow Chart

We map every field, trigger, webhook payload, and branching condition visually. We account for missing data fields, duplicate contact entries, and potential error states early.

Trigger & Payload SchemasDeduplication RulesBranching Decision Trees
STAGE 03

Design Workflow Architecture

Artifact: Architecture Blueprint

We establish structured data models in Airtable or HubSpot, define API rate limits, and construct clear architecture schematics that both technical and non-technical stakeholders can review.

Relational Database DesignCRM Property MappingError Handling Directives
STAGE 04

Build Scenarios & Integrations

Artifact: Production Scenario Modules

We engineer the scenarios in Make.com, GoHighLevel, or Zapier, embedding custom error handling routers, retry directives, and administrative alert channels.

Make.com / Zapier ModulesWebhook HandlersFallback Alert Webhooks
STAGE 05

Validate Under Real Scenarios

Artifact: Verification & Test Log

Every scenario undergoes live payload testing: rapid duplicate submissions, malformed email strings, and edge-case failure simulations to verify fail-safe behavior.

Edge-Case TestingTimeout & Retry VerificationPayload Integrity Checks
STAGE 06

Document & Operational Handoff

Artifact: Loom Guides & Handover Doc

We deliver recorded Loom video walkthroughs and architectural schematics so your team has complete clarity on how the system runs, monitors, and scales.

Recorded Loom WalkthroughsArchitecture SchematicsTeam Ownership Guides
