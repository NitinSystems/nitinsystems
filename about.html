ABOUT THE PRACTICE

About Nitin Mishra

B2B automation systems specialist operating a focused solo consulting practice.

Why Nitin Systems Exists

Nitin Systems is intentionally built as a direct, founder-led consulting practice. Modern businesses rarely suffer from a lack of software tools—they suffer from tools that do not speak to one another, lead data that decays, and processes that depend entirely on human memory.

My focus is designing and building the connective tissue: Make.com scenarios, Airtable database models, HubSpot and GoHighLevel CRM workflows, and reliable API integrations that keep business processes moving smoothly without friction.

How I Think About Systems

Automation isn't the goal; reliable execution is. A complex workflow with 50 nodes that breaks invisibly is worse than a simple 4-step process with bulletproof error routing. Every system I build is architected with clear visual documentation, edge-case testing, and clean handoffs.
