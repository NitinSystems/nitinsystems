Why Nitin Systems Exists

Nitin Systems is intentionally built as a direct, founder-led consulting practice. Modern businesses rarely suffer from a lack of software tools—they suffer from tools that do not speak to one another, lead data that decays, and processes that depend entirely on human memory.
